We buy silverSilver

Grandmother’s cutlery, the tray in the display cabinet, the candlestick nobody uses: we buy silver by fineness and weight, piece by piece or as a set.

Request a valuation What 800 and 925 mean

Four numbers we look for in the hallmark

Fineness shows, in parts per thousand, how much pure silver an item contains. Old Hungarian silver is usually 800, English and modern jewellery 925. Below are our indicative prices per gram (updated: 13 November 2026).

  • 800290-420 HUF/gold Hungarian and Austrian cutlery
  • 835310-440 HUF/gornaments, trays, candlesticks
  • 900330-480 HUF/gold silver coins
  • 925340-490 HUF/gsterling silver, jewellery

Indicative price; the final offer follows testing.

Based on the daily world market price and the exchange rate of the Hungarian National Bank (MNB).

The final offer comes after testing. If an item is worth more because of its decoration or its maker, we price it as a piece.

How much a set weighs

A traditional cutlery set for 12 can easily weigh 2-3 kilograms. The weights below are a guide; our scale gives the exact figure.

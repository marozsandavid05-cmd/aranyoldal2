What data we handle

  • In a message sent through the site: your name, phone number, the place you chose, the suggested time for a call back and what you write about the item.
  • On a phone call, on Viber and by email: what you tell us or write, and your phone number or email address.
  • For a house call: the address we visit and the agreed time.
  • When we buy: the details of the transaction.

What we use it for

To answer your enquiry, to arrange the valuation and the time of a house call, and to keep the purchase records required by law. We do not send newsletters, and we do not use your data for advertising.

Our legal basis

  • Enquiries and arranging a visit: your consent, or steps taken before entering into a contract.
  • Purchase records: legal obligation.

How long we keep it

Enquiries for no more than one year after they are closed. Purchase records for as long as the law requires.

Who we share it with

No one we are not required to. Authorities only under the law, on request. Our email is handled by Google’s email service (Gmail), so messages sent by email or through the form also pass through Google’s servers.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you which of your data we hold, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted unless the law forbids deletion. You can withdraw your consent at any time. You can complain to the Hungarian National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH, Falk Miksa utca 9-11, 1055 Budapest, naih.hu) or go to court.

Cookies and third-party services

This site uses no tracking or advertising cookies and does not measure visits.

On the Contact page, the map loads its tiles from the OpenFreeMap service. To do this, your browser connects to the provider’s server, which, as usual, receives your IP address and browser details. The map loads only when you scroll to it on the Contact page.

The details you enter in the message form (name, phone number, item and message) are forwarded to our email address by the Web3Forms form service (data processor). Web3Forms handles the message only to forward it; the site itself does not store any data.

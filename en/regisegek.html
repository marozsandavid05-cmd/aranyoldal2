A piece of porcelain, a painting, or a whole flat that has to be cleared now. We go through what is valuable and name a fair price for it.

What we look at

The mark under a piece of porcelain, the back of a painting, the drawer of a piece of furniture: these tell us when and where an item was made. We also buy fur coats, together with gold jewellery: fur coat buying.

Porcelain and ceramicsHerend, Zsolnay, Hollóháza, Meissen, Vienna

Figures, vases, dinner and coffee services. Incomplete sets and slightly chipped pieces count too.

Paintings and printsoil paintings, watercolours, etchings

Signed and unsigned works. We look at the frame and the back too, because the story is often there.

Ornamentsbronze, glass, silver-plated pieces

Sculpture, clocks, crystal, cigarette cases, writing instruments. If you do not know what it is, that is exactly why it is worth showing us.

Smaller furniturechests of drawers, display cabinets, bedside tables

We buy old pieces in good condition. For larger furniture, we first tell you from a photo whether it is worth bringing in.

When a whole flat is waiting

With an estate, the family rarely knows what is worth money and what is not. We do not rush, and we take nothing we have not agreed on.

  1. Phone

    You tell us roughly what is in the flat and when suits you.

  2. We come to you

    Free of charge in Budapest, at a time that suits you. We go through the flat in an hour or two.

  3. An itemised offer

    We price item by item or as a whole and explain what is worth what. You can think it over; there is no need to decide on the spot.

  4. Collection

    Once we agree, we pay and take the items away ourselves.

Booking a visit

An item often carries a family’s memories. That is why we show you everything we look at, and we never rush you.

How a valuation works

It takes a few minutes and commits you to nothing.

  1. 1 / 3

    We look at it.

    We examine the item in front of you: hallmark, material, condition. Without damaging it, at the counter or on your table.

  2. 2 / 3

    We weigh it.

    On a certified scale, to the gram. You see the weight before we name a price.

  3. 3 / 3

    We make an offer.

    We tell you how much we will pay for it, and why. If you accept, we pay on the spot. If not, the item stays with you, and you owe us nothing.

How a valuation works

A few minutes, in front of you, and it commits you to nothing. We show you everything we measure and tell you how we arrive at the price.

Request a valuation What to bring
  1. 1We look at it
  2. 2We test it
  3. 3We weigh it
  4. 4We make an offer

585

parts per thousand, 14-karat gold

1We look at it

We find the hallmark and look at the material and the condition, with a loupe if needed, at the counter or on your table. The item stays in front of you the whole time.

999.9

parts per thousand, pure gold

2We test it

If there is no hallmark, we test it without damage to see whether it really is gold or silver, and of what fineness. We show you the result.

0.1g

precision, and you see the display

3We weigh it

On a certified scale, to a tenth of a gram. We deduct stones, springs and every part that is not precious metal, and tell you how much.

HUF

in forints, on the spot

4We make an offer

We tell you how much we will pay and how we got there: fineness, weight, the day’s rate, or the item’s collector value. If you accept, we pay on the spot. If not, the item stays with you.

What to bring

Three things make the valuation quicker.

  1. The item, as it is.Do not clean or repair it first, and do not sort through the box.
  2. What comes with it.Box, certificate, receipt, old papers. If you have them, they help a lot.
  3. Anything you are unsure about.If you do not know what it is, that is exactly why it is worth showing us. The valuation is free and commits you to nothing.

Frequently asked questions

If your question is not answered here, call us: +36 20 620 1377

Do I need an appointment for a valuation?

You can walk into the shop during opening hours without an appointment. For a house call we agree a time by phone.

How much does a valuation cost?

Nothing. The valuation is free in the shop and on house calls in Budapest, even if you sell nothing.

What happens if I turn down the offer?

The item stays with you, and you owe us nothing. You can think it over and come back later.

How do you pay?

Straight after we agree, on the spot.

Can you give me a price over the phone?

Roughly, yes: tell us what it is, what hallmark you can see and roughly how much there is, and we will tell you what to expect. The exact offer comes after testing.

Will you come to me even if I only have one or two items?

Yes, even for a single ring. The visit is free in Budapest, and we agree the time by phone.

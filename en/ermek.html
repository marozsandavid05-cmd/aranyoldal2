Coins and decorations · we buy in Budapest

A box of old money, a carefully arranged album, or grandfather’s decoration. We look at each piece and tell you whether the metal or the collector value counts.

Request a valuation Before you bring them in

Two separate worlds

We value a coin either by the weight of its precious metal or as a collector’s piece, whichever is worth more to you.

Please do not clean your coins

Rubbing, polishing and chemical cleaning scratch the surface, and that can cut a collectible coin’s value to a fraction.

Bring them as they are, ideally in their original holders. If they are in an album, leave them in it.

Some common coins, at indicative prices

The price is a floor based on the precious metal content. We also buy more valuable pieces and serious numismatic dates: if a coin is a rarer date or in better condition, it can be worth several times this.

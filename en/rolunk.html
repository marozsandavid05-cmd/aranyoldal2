What we promise

Four tickets that go with every item, whether you bring a single ring or a whole estate.

  • You will see everything.

    The item stays in front of you the whole time. We show you the scale, the hallmark and the test result too.

  • We tell you why.

    With every offer we explain how we arrived at it: fineness, weight, the day’s rate, or a piece’s collector value.

  • We never rush you.

    If you want to think it over, take the item home. Our offer does not commit you to anything.

  • We come to you.

    If it is hard for you to come in, or a whole flat needs going through, we come to you free of charge in Budapest, at a time that suits you.

A small shop on the boulevard

The shop is at Teréz körút 35, between Oktogon and Nyugati railway station. A small shop with one counter and one certified scale. For forty years we have done the same thing here: we look, we weigh and we name a fair price.

Many people come in with a single ring, others call about an estate. We give both the same time.

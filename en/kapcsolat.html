How to find us

On the boulevard, look for the black sign with gold lettering. The panels on either side of the door show what we buy, and the opening hours are on the glass.

Between Oktogon and Nyugati railway station, a few minutes’ walk from the Oktogon or Nyugati pályaudvar stops of trams 4 and 6. By metro, M1 Oktogon and M3 Nyugati pályaudvar are also close.
